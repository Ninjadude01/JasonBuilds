# JasonBuilds
Website Link: https://ninjadude01.github.io/JasonBuilds/

This is my personal website, it acts as a small snippet into my life! It's written using HTML and CSS, and deployed through github.

This is my first time making a website, however I am still very proud about how it turned out.
When you open my website up, there is a navigation bar. There are three unique pages: a home/welcome page, an about me, and a projects! 

In the home page, I have included my favorite song which you can play yourself, and a cool photo of me at Gardens by the Bay in Singapore. In the about me page, I wrote a description about how I got into hardware/electronics, and included many photos of past hackathons I have attended. Finally, on the project section, I included three hardware projects that I have designed over the past few months, along with their github repos.

I hope you enjoy looking through my website! Feel free to contact me if you have any questions.
