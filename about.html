About Me
My passion for engineering and electronics started back in December of 2025, when my friend asked me to attend a game jam they were organizing (a hackathon where teens code and build their own games from scratch). I had no prior experience, however, I was so interested that I asked to help organize the event instead. This was how I found out about Hackclub (since they were helping fund the game jam), a non-profit that aims to empower teens to learn coding and hardware engineering.

I then learned Hack Club was also running a hardware hackathon in Texas; however, to qualify, I had to design and build hardware projects. After CADing up some basic designs and learning how to create simple PCB's, I fell in love with hardware, and I have been working on cooler and more ambitious projects ever since.

I also enjoy playing video games, working out at the gym, and hanging out with my friends! I'm on my school's robotics team, where I do a lot of the CAD and mechanical work. I am familiar with C++, Java, HTML, CSS, KiCad, Fusion 360 and many other languages/applications.
