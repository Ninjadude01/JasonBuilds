Projects

Here are some projects I have made (some are currently being built IRL):

Ninjaboard65

A 65% custom keyboard with a knob! Features include hot-swappable gateron yellow switches, pbt double-shot keycaps, and a 3d printed case + plate.

NinjaArm

NinjaArm is a custom robotic arm with 3 degrees of freedom + a claw. It uses an Arduino Uno along with a servo board, 3 servos, 1 micro servo, and 4 potentiometers to control rotation.

NinjapadV2

The Ninjapad-V2 is a wireless macropad with 6 switches, a customizable knob, and a 0.91" oled display! It is fully hot-swappable, uses a 3.7V 600mAh rechargable lithium battery, and has mini-leds.
