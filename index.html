Welcome!

Hi there, welcome to my personal website! My name is Jason Liu, and I am a 17 year old student who loves building cool hardware and software projects in their free time. I currently attend Westdale Secondary School, and I hope to go to Waterloo University in the near future to become a mechatronics engineer.

About Me

My passion for engineering and electronics started back in December of 2025, when my best friend asked me to attend a game jam they were organizing. I had no prior experience, however I was so interested that I asked to help organize the event instead. This was a global game jam run by Hackclub, a non-profit that aims to empower teens to learn coding and hardware engineering.

Hack club was also running a hardware hackathon in Texas, however to qualify I had to design and build hardware projects. After CADing up and learning to wire some basic projects for this event, I fell in love with hardware, and I have been working on cooler and more ambitious projects ever since.

I also enjoy playing video games, working out at the gym, and hanging out with my friends! I'm on my school robotics team, and I do lots of the CAD + mechanical work. I am familiar with C++, Java, HTML, CSS, KiCad, Fusion 360 and many other languages/applications.

Projects

Here are some projects I am working on:

Ninjaboard65

A 65% custom keyboard with a knob! Features include hot-swappable gateron yellow switches and a 3d printed case + plate that was designed in Fusion 360. (April to June 2026)


NinjaArm

NinjaArm is a custom robotic arm with 3 degrees of freedom + a claw. It uses an Arduino Uno along with a servo board, 3 servos, 1 micro servo, and 4 potentiometers to control rotation. (April 2026 - Current Day)

NinjapadV2

The Ninjapad-V2 is a wireless macropad with 6 switches, a customizable knob, and a 0.91" oled display! It is fully hot-swappable, uses a 3.7V 600mAh rechargable lithium battery, and has mini-leds. (June 2026 - Current Day)
