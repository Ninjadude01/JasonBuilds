About Me

Hello there! My name is Jason, a 17 year old who loves working with electronics and hardware.
My journey started back in December of 2025, when my best friend asked me to attend a game jam they were organizing. I had no prior experience, however I was so interested that I asked to help organize the event instead. This was a global game jam happening during the same weekend in over 200 cities, run by a non-profit called Hackclub. They also were running a hackathon hardware hackathon in Texas, however to qualify I had to design and build hardware projects. After CADing up and learning to wire some basic projects, I fell in love with hardware, and I have been working on bigger and better projects ever since.
I also enjoy playing video games, working out at the gym, and hanging out with my friends! I am familiar with C++, Java, HTML, and CSS. I also know how to use KiCad and Fusion 360.

Projects

Here are some projects I am working on:

Ninjaboard65

A 65% custom keyboard with a knob! Features include hot-swappable gateron yellow switches and a 3d printed case + plate that was designed in Fusion 360. (April to June 2026)


NinjaArm

NinjaArm is a custom robotic arm with 3 degrees of freedom + a claw. It uses an Arduino Uno along with a servo board, 3 servos, 1 micro servo, and 4 potentiometers to control rotation. (May 2026 - Current Day)

NinjapadV2

The Ninjapad-V2 is a wireless macropad with 6 switches, a customizable knob, and a 0.91" oled display! It is fully hot-swappable, uses a 3.7V 600mAh rechargable lithium battery, and has mini-leds. (July 2026 - Current Day)
